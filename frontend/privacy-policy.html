Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information MMC ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and how it's handled. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, and a small amount of technical information collected automatically:

  • Account information — name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text) when you register for an account.
  • Contact form submissions — name, email, phone number, and message when you contact us.
  • Newsletter signups — email address when you subscribe.
  • Technical data — IP address and timestamps, used for spam prevention and rate limiting on form submissions.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you when you log in.
  • Respond to messages sent through the contact form.
  • Send newsletter updates to subscribers, roughly once a month.
  • Detect and prevent spam or abuse of our forms.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data storage and security

Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. Account sessions are managed using signed tokens. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Cookies and tracking

This site does not use tracking cookies. If analytics are enabled in the future, we intend to use privacy-respecting tools that do not track individuals across sites.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below. If you have an account, you can also unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@mmc-group.com or through our contact page.
